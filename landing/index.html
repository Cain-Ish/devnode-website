The person behind the unicorn

The "unicorn" label started as a joke on my old link page — the rare frontend person who's equally at home in component APIs, accessibility trees, and roadmap meetings. It stuck, because that mix is exactly what I offer.

I've spent [PLACEHOLDER: 10+] years building for the web, the recent ones as a senior.
